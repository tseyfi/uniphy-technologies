Interference mitigation / 2026 preprint

AIIM:
coordinate heterogeneous 5G cells.

Samuel Reinders · Alireza Ebrahimi Dorcheh · Ryan Barker · Tolunay Seyfi · Fatemeh Afghah

AIIM is an adaptive inter-cell interference mitigation xApp for multi-vendor 5G O-RAN. It coordinates PRB allocation across heterogeneous base stations and evaluates PPO and DQN policies on a full-stack platform with SDR and virtual gNBs and UEs.

Read the paper ↗Discuss a demonstration
Demo concept

Make interference visible.

Run a heterogeneous multi-cell O-RAN testbed, vary user placement and traffic demand, and compare PPO and DQN xApps with static and semi-static interference-mitigation policies.

Key results

Adaptive policies improve QoS

PPO and DQN outperform the reported traditional static and semi-static policies across user placements and traffic conditions.

QoS can trade against aggregate throughput

Learning policies may show slightly lower aggregate throughput in some cases because they serve high-pathloss users more fairly while reducing quality-of-service violations.

Full-stack testbed

The evaluation combines SDR-based and virtual gNBs and UEs with O-RAN software components, 15-second iPerf3 traffic, and 350 evaluation steps.
