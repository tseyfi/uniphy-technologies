O-RAN security / 2026 preprint

ORAN-DEFEND:
sanitize compromised xApps.

Md Raihan Uddin · Fatemeh Lotfi · Tolunay Seyfi · Fatemeh Afghah

A retraining-free defense for backdoored deep reinforcement learning xApps in the near-real-time RIC. ORAN-DEFEND learns a trusted KPI subspace from clean calibration windows, then projects suspicious observations back into that safe geometry before the policy acts.

Read the paper ↗Discuss a demonstration
Demo concept

Detect, sanitize, and compare.

Simulate a compromised DRL xApp, inject KPI backdoor triggers, and compare clean, attacked, and sanitized reward trajectories. The live version can connect the same workflow to Near-RT RIC and E2 telemetry.

Key results

100% recovery on E⊥ triggers

Across four backdoor attack families, the projected defense restores the return to the clean baseline on the evaluated track.

Low calibration cost

Recovery stays within 0.05 of the clean baseline, with regret below 1e-3 even at eight calibration samples.

Geometry matters

Recovery follows the trigger energy outside the trusted subspace. Triggers aligned with the learned subspace remain a known limitation.
