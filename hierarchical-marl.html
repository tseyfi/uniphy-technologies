RAN control / 2026 preprint

Hierarchical cooperative
control for 5G resources.

Alireza Ebrahimi Dorcheh · Tolunay Seyfi · Ryan Barker · Fatemeh Afghah

A staged multi-agent reinforcement-learning framework for joint downlink PRB and power allocation. A PRB agent sets user quotas while a power agent resolves the continuous allocation problem, using Sionna and Sionna RT for channel and PHY/MAC evaluation.

Read the paper ↗Discuss a demonstration
Demo concept

Allocate resources in a ray-traced world.

Create an urban scene, vary users and channels, run hierarchical PRB-plus-power control, and compare throughput and Jain fairness against a proportional-fair equal-power baseline and ablations.

Key results

Throughput–fairness operating point

The joint PRB and power policy reports the strongest throughput improvement in the matched-channel evaluation, with a modest reduction in Jain fairness.

Structured action space

Quota decisions are resolved into exact PRB assignments before power control, reducing the combinatorial burden of directly learning every discrete and continuous variable together.

Simulation grounded in propagation

Sionna RT supplies mobility-aware channels while the PHY/MAC loop returns throughput, fairness, HARQ, and link-adaptation signals.
