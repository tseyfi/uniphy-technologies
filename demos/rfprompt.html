RFPrompt:
adapt a wireless model with prompts.

Md Raihan Uddin · Tolunay Seyfi · Fatemeh Afghah

A signal-intelligence demonstration that adapts a frozen large wireless model with small expert prompts, then tests modulation classification with limited labels and shifted signal conditions.

Read the paper ↗Discuss a demonstration
Demo concept

Change the label budget. See adaptation.

Upload IQ samples, choose modulation classes, and compare a frozen model, partial fine-tuning, and RFPrompt as labels and signal conditions change.

What the demo shows

Strong OOD performance

On the Real-World IQ test set, RFPrompt peaks at 0.82 accuracy at N=400, exceeding the reported frozen and partially fine-tuned baselines.

Parameter efficient

The prompt-based adaptation uses about 0.34% trainable parameters in the reported comparison, while keeping the backbone frozen.

Prompt length sensitivity

The reported sweep identifies P=16 as the best prompt length for the evaluated adaptation setting.
